Donde Empiezan los Sueños Encontraran Nuestras Huellas (2016 - Ongoing)

2016 · Globally Personal series 17 photographs

Donde Empiezan los Sueños Encontrarán Nuestras Huellas (2016 - Ongoing) is a photography project started in 2016, where I explore the seaside, the people that visit it, the objects they bring and the structures that are raised on it for human necessities. Through this ongoing project I try to explore landscape, placing the question of whether the pasts, presents and futures of landscapes can be represented through images? And trying to understand whether it is the seaside which attracts people to it, or the idea of temporarily occupying it.
