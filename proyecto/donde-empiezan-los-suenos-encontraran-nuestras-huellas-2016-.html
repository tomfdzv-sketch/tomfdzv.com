Donde Empiezan los Sueños Encontraran Nuestras Huellas (2016 - Ongoing)

2016 · Globally Serie personal 21 fotografías

Donde Empiezan los Sueños Encontraran Nuestras Huellas (2016 - Ongoing) is a photography project started in 2016, where I explore the seaside, the people that visit it, the objects they bring and the structures that are raised on it for human necessities. Through this ongoing project I try to explore landscape, placing the question of wether the pasts, presents and futures of landscapes can be represented through images?And trying to understand wether if it is the seaside which attracts people to it, or the idea of temporarily occupying it.
